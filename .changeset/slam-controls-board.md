`SlamControls` matches its board. From 672 px wide the map legend starts collapsed, through a new optional `legendOpen` prop on `SlamMap2D`. The End position fields are named "End position X, meters" and "End position Y, meters". Execute is disabled while a MoveOnMap call is in flight, and Stop never is. The control's recommended widths are 320 and 672 px, and it grows to 896 px.
