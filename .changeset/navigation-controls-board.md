`NavigationServiceControls` matches its board. A service whose map type is None shows a notice in the map's place, with Focus and click-to-add off. Paths from `GetPaths` draw as lines on the map. Box obstacle sizes print to one decimal. Mode is disabled while a SetMode call is in flight, and each waypoint's Remove while its own remove is in flight.
